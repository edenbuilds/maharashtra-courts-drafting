# Court fees and stamp — Maharashtra

Always re-check the latest notification. Figures below are orientation, not a fee calculator.

## Court fees
- **Act:** Bombay Court-Fees Act, 1959 (as applicable to Maharashtra), plus High Court / Government notifications.
- Writs and most criminal applications at the High Court: fixed or slab — read Schedule I / II against the current year.
- First appeals: ad valorem on the decree value unless a special statute (MV Act MACT appeals have a distinct treatment plus the s.173(2) deposit).
- MACT claims: nominal slab; exemption applications are common for minors, widows and persons below poverty line.
- Consumer: Central fee table under CPA 2019 (District: nil up to Rs.5 lakh; then stepped; State and NCDRC separate).
- DRT OA: fee per the RDDBFI Fee Rules as amended.
- MAT OA: fee per the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 1988 / current GAD notification.

## Statutory deposits
- MV Act s.173(2): insurer / owner deposit Rs.25,000 or 50 percent of the award, whichever is less, unless exempted by the Court. Annex the challan.
- Consumer / DRT / RERA appeals: read the parent statute for pre-deposit. Do not invent a percentage.

## Stamp (instruments, not pleadings)
- **Act:** Maharashtra Stamp Act, 1958 + annual rate card of the Inspector General of Registration, Maharashtra.
- Conveyances, gifts, mortgages, partitions, development agreements, leave-and-licence: ready-reckoner of the relevant year × prescribed percentage. Ready-reckoner is district-specific (Mumbai City / Suburban tables differ from Pune / Thane).
- Article numbers move by notification. The deed skills in this pack name the article family, not a frozen rupee figure.

## Advocate welfare / enrolment
Vakalatnama in the State commonly carries an advocate welfare stamp as notified by the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa. Put the enrolment number on the counsel block when the forum-config says so.
