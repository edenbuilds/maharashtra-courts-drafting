# Territorial jurisdiction — Maharashtra

Confirm sitting notifications on the official portal before filing. Dual place-names: Aurangabad / Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar; Ahmednagar / Ahilyanagar; Osmanabad / Dharashiv. Use the form the forum currently prints on cause lists.

## High Court of Judicature at Bombay

### Principal Seat (Mumbai)
Mumbai City, Mumbai Suburban, Thane, Palghar, Nashik, Pune, Raigad, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, Daman, Diu.

Kolhapur circuit (notified August 2025; confirm whether the circuit is sitting for the cause-list week): Satara, Sangli, Solapur, Kolhapur, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg. If the circuit is not sitting, those districts remain with the Principal Seat.

Original Side (admiralty, testamentary, company transferred leftovers, high-value commercial OS, Parsi Matrimonial, etc.) is **Mumbai only**.

### Nagpur Bench
Nagpur, Akola, Amravati, Bhandara, Buldhana, Chandrapur, Wardha, Yavatmal, Gondia, Gadchiroli, Washim.

### Aurangabad Bench
Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Ahilyanagar, Beed, Dhule, Jalna, Jalgaon, Latur, Nanded, Dharashiv, Parbhani, Nandurbar, Hingoli.

### Goa (same High Court, not a Maharashtra district forum)
North Goa, South Goa. Do not route Maharashtra district matters here.

## Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal
- **Mumbai (Principal):** Konkan-facing districts including Mumbai, Thane, Palghar, Raigad, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg (confirm latest territorial notification).
- **Nagpur:** Vidarbha districts mirroring the Nagpur HC map.
- **Aurangabad:** Marathwada plus notified Nashik-division districts such as Dhule (Government notification under AT Act s.16 — verify current list).

## DRT / DRAT (debt threshold matters)
- **DRT-1 Mumbai:** notified high-value bucket (applications of Rs.100 crore and above drawing from the western cluster — verify SO 4717(E) / successor).
- **DRT-2 / DRT-3 Mumbai:** Mumbai and residual Konkan matters below the high-value cut-off.
- **DRT Pune:** Pune, Satara, Sangli, Kolhapur, Solapur, Raigad, Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg (below high-value cut-off).
- **DRT Nagpur:** Vidarbha list.
- **DRT Aurangabad:** Marathwada + Ahilyanagar, Jalgaon, Dhule, Nandurbar.
- **DRAT Mumbai:** appeals from the western DRTs including Gujarat DRTs attached to this DRAT — check the appeal memo against the latest attachment notification.

## Consumer
- District Commission: district of cause of action / opposite party residence / branch (CPA 2019 s.34).
- State Commission, Maharashtra: principal at Mumbai; regional / circuit benches at Nagpur and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.
- Pecuniary (verify latest Central notification): District up to Rs.50 lakh; State above Rs.50 lakh to Rs.2 crore; NCDRC above Rs.2 crore.

## MACT
File where the accident occurred, or where the claimant resides, or where the defendant resides (MV Act s.166(2)). Appeal under s.173 lies to the Bombay High Court bench that has territorial control over that MACT district.

## NCLT
NCLT Mumbai Bench for companies whose registered office is in Maharashtra (and the attached Union Territory slice as notified). Appeals go to NCLAT, not to the Bombay High Court as a first appellate forum.

## Labour / Industrial
Labour Court and Industrial Tribunal under the ID Act follow the State labour-department notification of local limits. Unfair labour practice and union recognition under the MRTU & PULP Act, 1971 go to the **Industrial Court** (Maharashtra-only forum), not to the Labour Court.
