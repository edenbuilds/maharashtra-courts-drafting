# E-filing and paper filing — Maharashtra

Portals change URLs. Confirm on the live site the week you file.

## High Court of Judicature at Bombay
- Appellate Side and Original Side have separate e-filing stacks on the official High Court portal.
- Practice Notes govern bookmarking of PDF annexures, OCR text layer, maximum file size, and naming (`WP_1234_2026_Annexure_A.pdf` style — follow the current Practice Note, do not invent a scheme).
- Urgent circulation / mentioning still follows the day's sitting list and the concerned Registrar (Judicial).
- Neutral citation and CNR appear on orders; quote them exactly when relying on a prior Bombay HC order.

## District judiciary / MACT / Family Court
- eCourts / e-Filing 3.0 for the district judiciary.
- MACT petitions in Mumbai and Pune are commonly e-filed; interior districts may still accept paper-first. Ask the filing clerk if the skill output will be printed or uploaded.

## Consumer
- e-daakhil (Central Consumer portal) is the default electronic path. State Commission Maharashtra also accepts physical filing at Mumbai / regional benches as per its latest circular.

## DRT / DRAT
- DRT e-filing portal under the Department of Financial Services. OA, SA (s.17), reply, and written submissions each have prescribed form numbers. Do not re-badge an OA as a writ.

## NCLT
- NCLT e-filing portal. IBC applications use the prescribed forms (Form 1 for s.7, Form 5 for s.9, etc.). Attach the master data print of the corporate debtor.

## MAT
- Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal has its own cause-list and filing counters at Mumbai, Nagpur and Aurangabad. Check whether e-filing is open for that bench that week.

## MahaRERA
- MahaRERA complaint is an online form plus uploaded PDF. The skill output is the narrative + prayer annex that gets pasted / uploaded; it is not a High Court writ.
