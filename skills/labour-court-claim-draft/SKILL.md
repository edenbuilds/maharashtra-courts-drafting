# Labour Court / Industrial Tribunal claim

Route Schedule II matters to the Labour Court and Schedule III to the Industrial Tribunal. Plead the employer-employee relationship, the industry, the workman status, and the demand. Reference under ID Act s.10 is a different instrument — say which one the user asked for.
