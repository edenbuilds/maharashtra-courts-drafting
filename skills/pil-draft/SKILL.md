# Public Interest Litigation

**Rules:** Bombay High Court Public Interest Litigation Rules, 2010.

Plead locus (why the petitioner may speak for the class), prior representations to the authority, absence of private motive, and the precise public duty. Attach the PIL disclosure affidavit the Rules require. Do not convert a private service or property dispute into a PIL.
