# Rejoinder Affidavit — Writ Petition

**Forum:** Same seat as the writ. **Side:** Petitioner only, and only after a reply is on record.

## Structure
1. Cause title of the pending writ. Identify the reply being answered (respondent number + date).
2. Confine to **new** facts and **new** preliminary objections in the reply. Do not re-argue the writ.
3. Meet each fresh objection briefly with the annexure that answers it.
4. Short prayer: that the preliminary objections be rejected and the petition be heard on merits / interim relief continued.
5. Verification. Petitioner annexures continue the petition scheme or use a clear rejoinder prefix stated once.

## Do not
- File a rejoinder that is only rhetoric when the reply added nothing new — flag that to the advocate.
- Treat the rejoinder as a second writ.
