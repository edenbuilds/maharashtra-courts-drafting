# MahaRERA complaint / MahaREAT appeal

Plead registration number of the project, promoter, booked flat / shop, dates of agreement and promised possession, amounts paid, and the s.18 / s.12 / s.14 relief actually attracted. Appeals go to MahaREAT, not to the High Court as a first statutory appeal.
