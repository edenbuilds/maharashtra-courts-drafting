# Civil Writ Petition — Articles 226 / 227

**Forum:** Bombay High Court bench mapped from the district of the impugned authority / order.
**Side:** Appellate Side, unless the matter is on the Original Side at Mumbai (rare for writs).

## When to use
Challenge to a State / local / statutory authority, service-law order **outside** MAT's exclusive cover, planning permission, education, cooperative, revenue (after statutory remedies where they are equally efficacious), certiorari against a quasi-judicial tribunal where the High Court is the correct forum.

## Do not use when
- The applicant is a Maharashtra State servant and the grievance is a "service matter" under s.15 of the Administrative Tribunals Act — route to MAT first unless the exception in L. Chandra Kumar / later law on judicial review after the tribunal is being invoked, in which case say so.
- An equally efficacious statutory appeal is open and no exception (natural justice, vires, pure question of law, futility) is pleaded.

## Structure
1. Header + writ number blank + "DISTRICT: [district]".
2. Cause title. Public authorities first as respondents; private parties only if necessary.
3. Statutory opening: "Petition under Article 226 [and Article 227] of the Constitution of India."
4. Synopsis. List of dates.
5. Facts with annexure markers.
6. Grounds, typically grouped as:
   - want of jurisdiction / ultra vires
   - violation of Article 14 / 19 / 21 as attracted
   - failure of natural justice
   - patent illegality / perversity
   - disproportionality
   - alternative remedy not equally efficacious (must be a pleaded paragraph, not a footnote)
7. Prayer: certiorari / mandamus / prohibition / quo warranto / declaration + stay + catch-all.
8. Interim relief application (separate) almost always travels with the writ.

## Bombay HC notes
Name the specific authority. Produce the impugned order as ANNEXURE-A unless chronology forces another letter. If a statutory pre-deposit or time-bar point exists, meet it in facts and grounds.
