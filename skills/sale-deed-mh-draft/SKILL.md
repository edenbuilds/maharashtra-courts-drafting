# Sale deed — Maharashtra

Consideration, ready-reckoner comparison, payment schedule, title recitals, encumbrance warranty, possession, and schedule of property in the registration office's language (often Marathi schedule + English deed, or bilingual). Stamp on the higher of consideration and ready-reckoner. Do not quote a stale percentage.
