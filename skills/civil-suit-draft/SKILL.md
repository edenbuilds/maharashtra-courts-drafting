# Civil plaint

Order VII CPC essentials: cause of action paragraph, valuation, court fee, jurisdiction paragraph, limitation paragraph. Verification under Order VI rule 15. List of documents. Specific-relief plaints must meet the 2018 amendments (readiness and willingness as a pleaded fact, not a slogan).
