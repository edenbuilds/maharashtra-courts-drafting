# MACT claim — s.166

Header of the district MACT. Parties: claimants, driver, owner, insurer (and employer if vicarious). Include the 22-item tabular particulars used in Maharashtra practice (identity, income, accident, vehicle, policy, FIR, injury / death, disability, heads of claim, interest, court fee).

Heads of claim follow Pranay Sethi / Sarla Verma / later binding law the user supplies — do not invent a multiplier. Hit-and-run solatium is s.161, not s.166. No-fault lump sum is s.164. Do not mix the three without a pleaded alternative.
