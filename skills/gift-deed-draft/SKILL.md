# Gift deed — Maharashtra

Recitals of ownership, relationship, voluntariness, delivery of possession, and acceptance by the donee (acceptance is essential). Stamp under the live Maharashtra article for gift (residential vs other, family vs stranger). Registration is mandatory for immovable property. Annex the latest property card / 7/12 extract / index II.
