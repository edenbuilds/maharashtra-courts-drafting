# Family Court petition

Name the exact section (HMA s.13, s.9, s.24, SMA equivalent, DV s.12, GWA). Plead the marriage / relationship, the children, the assets if relevant, and the statutory ground in the statute's words. Mediation is the default first listing in most Maharashtra Family Courts — do not draft as if the matter will be tried on day one.
