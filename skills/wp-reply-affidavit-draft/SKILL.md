# Affidavit in Reply — Writ Petition

**Forum:** Same Bombay High Court seat as the writ.
**Side:** Respondent. Title the paper **Affidavit in Reply** (Bombay usage). "Counter-affidavit" only as a subtitle if the brief uses that word.

## Structure
1. Cause title of the pending writ. State deponent capacity and authorisation (ANNEXURE-R-1).
2. General denial. Then **preliminary objections** that actually fit: alternative remedy, delay/laches, necessary party, disputed facts, suppression. Strike empty lettered clauses.
3. Paragraph-to-paragraph reply to the petition facts. Admit / deny / qualify. Do not invent a parallel narrative.
4. Reply to grounds only where a new legal answer is needed.
5. Prayer: dismiss the petition; vacate ad-interim relief if any; costs.
6. Verification. List of respondent annexures as **ANNEXURE-R-1…**.

## Do not
- Convert the reply into a fresh writ or counter-claim.
- Keep unused preliminary-objection limbs.
- Invent citations, fees or limitation articles.
