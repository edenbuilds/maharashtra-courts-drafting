# DRT Original Application

Choose DRT-1/2/3 Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur or Aurangabad from the territorial + pecuniary map. Plead the debt, the documents of title, the limitation computation, and the relief (recovery certificate). This is not a civil suit and not a writ.
