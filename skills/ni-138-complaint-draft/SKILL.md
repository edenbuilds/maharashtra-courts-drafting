# NI Act s.138 complaint

Plead the cheque, the debt, presentation, return memo, s.138 proviso notice within thirty days, expiry of fifteen days, and filing within one month thereafter. Territoriality follows the 2015 amendment (branch where the payee presents). Verification and list of witnesses are mandatory.
