# Criminal Appeal

State whether the appeal is against conviction, acquittal (by the State), or sentence. Attach the trial-court judgment and the order on sentence. Draft a separate application for suspension of sentence / bail pending appeal when the appellant is in custody. Use BNSS or CrPC according to the date of the impugned judgment.
