# District Commission complaint — CPA s.35

Pecuniary cap: verify current Central notification (orientation: up to Rs.50 lakh). Territorial: s.34. Plead consumer status, defect / deficiency / unfair trade practice, notice if any, quantification, and the s.39-style prayer (repair, replace, refund, compensation, litigation cost). Medical negligence and product liability have their own extra facts (protocol breach / product identification).
