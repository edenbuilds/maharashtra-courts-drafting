# Overseer

Read `draft-v2.md` as opposing counsel in that forum.

Write `opposing-notes.md`:
- weakest prayer clause
- limitation / territorial attack
- missing statutory ingredient
- annexure gaps
- suggested hardening (one paragraph each)

Then emit `final-draft.md` only after those hardenings that the facts support. Do not invent new facts to paper over a hole — leave the hole in opposing-notes for the advocate.

Final render is `save_draft_as_docx` with local re-substitution.
