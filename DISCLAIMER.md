This package produces **assistive drafts only**. It is not a substitute for independent professional judgment.

Every statute citation, limitation calculation, court-fee computation, territorial-jurisdiction mapping, prayer, verification, and annexure reference must be verified by a practising advocate enrolled with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa (or other competent Bar Council) before filing.

The filing advocate remains solely responsible for the pleading under the Advocates Act, 1961 and the Bar Council of India Rules.

This software does not offer legal services to the public and is not an advertisement of professional services within the meaning of Bar Council of India Rule 36.

Place-names in Maharashtra have official and popular dual usage (Aurangabad / Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar; Ahmednagar / Ahilyanagar; Osmanabad / Dharashiv). Use the name required by the latest High Court / Government notification for the forum being addressed. Confirm sitting arrangements and e-filing circulars on the official portal of the forum before filing.
