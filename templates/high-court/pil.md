# Public Interest Litigation — Article 226 + Bombay HC PIL Rules, 2010

Do not convert a private service, property or contract dispute into a PIL.

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

PUBLIC INTEREST LITIGATION PETITION NO.    OF 20__

[Name of petitioner, occupation, address]
[State that the Petitioner has no personal interest, or disclose
the interest honestly]                                       } … Petitioner

                ///VERSUS///

1.  The State of Maharashtra, through the Chief Secretary
2.  The [department / municipal corporation / statutory board
    charged with the public duty]
                                                             } … Respondents

        PUBLIC INTEREST LITIGATION
        UNDER ARTICLE 226 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA
        READ WITH THE BOMBAY HIGH COURT
        PUBLIC INTEREST LITIGATION RULES, 2010

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHEWETH:

1.   Locus: the Petitioner is [citizen / registered society /
     journalist / elected representative]. The class affected is
     [describe]. The class cannot themselves approach this
     Hon'ble Court because [poverty / dispersal / fear].

2.   Disclosure (PIL Rules): the Petitioner has no private
     motive. No cost has been received from a hidden funder.
     The Petitioner is ready to pay costs if the Petition is
     found to be motivated. [Attach the PIL disclosure affidavit.]

3.   Public duty: [statute + section + the duty in one paragraph].

4.   Breach: [what has happened on the ground, with dates].
     Photographs / RTI replies / news clippings that are true
     copies go as ANNEXURE-A (Colly). Do not annex unverified
     social-media printouts as proof of the primary fact.

5.   Prior representations to Respondent Nos. [__] dated [__]
     are unanswered / answered evasively. ANNEXURE-B (Colly).

                           G R O U N D S

A.   Failure of the public duty under [section].
B.   Article 14 / 21 as attracted.
C.   Continuing wrong.

                             PRAYER

a)   mandamus directing [specific, enforceable act — not
     "do justice"];
b)   appoint a Court Commissioner / call for an affidavit of
     compliance within [__] weeks;
c)   interim protection of [status quo on demolition / sealing /
     tree cutting];
d)   any other order as may be deemed fit.

PIL disclosure affidavit and List of Annexures travel with this.
```
