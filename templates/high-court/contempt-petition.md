# Contempt Petition — Contempt of Courts Act, 1971

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

CONTEMPT PETITION NO.                OF 20__
IN
[PARENT WRIT / APPEAL NO. __ OF 20__]

[Petitioner]                                         } … Petitioner

                ///VERSUS///

[Alleged contemnor — name and designation]           } … Respondent

        CONTEMPT PETITION
        UNDER SECTION 12 OF THE CONTEMPT OF COURTS ACT, 1971
        READ WITH ARTICLE 215 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA


1.   By order dated [__] in [parent case number] this Hon'ble
     Court directed the Respondent to [exact operative words].
     A certified copy is ANNEXURE-A.

2.   The order was served / the Respondent was present when it
     was pronounced, on [date]. ANNEXURE-B.

3.   The Respondent has [not complied / complied in a manner
     that defeats the direction], in that [facts after the order].
     Correspondence is ANNEXURE-C (Colly).

4.   The disobedience is wilful.

                             PRAYER

a)   initiate proceedings for contempt against the Respondent;
b)   punish the Respondent in accordance with the Contempt of
     Courts Act, 1971;
c)   direct compliance of the order dated [__] forthwith;
d)   any other order as may be deemed fit.

Do not prescribe the sentence. Civil and criminal contempt are
different — pick one and plead the mental element that matches.
```
