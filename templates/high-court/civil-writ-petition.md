# Civil Writ Petition — Articles 226 / 227

Forum: High Court of Judicature at Bombay. Side: Appellate Side unless the matter is on the Original Side at Mumbai.

Pack with: Index, Synopsis, List of Annexures (`00-index-synopsis-annexures.md`), Vakalatnama, and — almost always — a separate Stay Application (`applications/stay-application.md`).

Do not use this form for a Maharashtra State service matter in the first instance. That goes to the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal.

---
