# Affidavit in Reply to a Writ Petition

Opposite-side draft. At the Bombay High Court this is titled **Affidavit in Reply**. "Counter-affidavit" is used in some other courts and may be kept as a subtitle if the brief uses that word. Do not convert a reply into a fresh writ.

Respond paragraph-to-paragraph. A general denial in one block is not a reply.

State respondents file through the Government Pleader / Public Prosecutor panel as applicable. Private respondents file through their own advocate.

Pack with: Index of reply annexures (prefix **R-1, R-2…** or **ANNEXURE-R-1** — pick one scheme and keep it), Vakalatnama if the respondent is appearing for the first time, and any application for vacating ad-interim relief.

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR]
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY
BENCH AT [NAGPUR / AURANGABAD / KOLHAPUR].

CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

WRIT PETITION NO. [____] OF 20__

[Name of Petitioner]                                 … Petitioner

                ///VERSUS///

[Name of Respondent No. 1 and others]                … Respondents


        AFFIDAVIT IN REPLY
        ON BEHALF OF RESPONDENT NO. [__]


I, [full name of deponent], aged about [__] years,
[occupation / official designation],
presently posted as [designation] in the office of [department /
authority], having office at [Address-Placeholder],
do hereby solemnly affirm and state on oath as under:


1.   I am the [Respondent No. __ / officer authorised to depose
     on behalf of Respondent No. __]. I have seen the Writ Petition
     and the annexures. I am conversant with the facts. I am
     competent to depose. A copy of the authorisation / office
     order, if required by the Registry, is annexed as ANNEXURE-R-1.

2.   At the outset I deny all allegations, contentions and grounds
     in the Petition save those that are specifically admitted
     herein. Nothing in the Petition may be treated as admitted
     for want of traverse.

3.   I say that the Petition is not maintainable and is liable to
     be dismissed in limine, without prejudice to the reply on
     merits, on the following preliminary objections:

     a)    The Petitioner has an equally efficacious statutory
           remedy under [section] of [statute], which has not been
           exhausted / has been abandoned.

     b)    The Petition suffers from delay and laches. The
           impugned [order] is dated [__]. The Petition was
           affirmed on [__]. No explanation is pleaded.

     c)    Necessary parties have not been joined, namely [__].

     d)    The Petition raises disputed questions of fact which
           cannot be tried on affidavits.

     e)    The Petitioner has suppressed [material fact — identify
           the document].

     [Strike unused objections. Do not keep empty lettered clauses.]


                       REPLY TO THE FACTS

4.   With reference to paragraph 1 of the Petition, I say that
     [admit identity / deny capacity / qualify].

5.   With reference to paragraph 2 of the Petition, I say that
     [the description of the Respondents is admitted, save that …].

6.   With reference to paragraph 3 of the Petition, I say that
     the document marked ANNEXURE-A is [admitted as a true copy
     of the order dated __ / not a complete copy / is an internal
     note and not an order].

7.   With reference to paragraph [__] of the Petition, I say that
     [the true chronology is as follows].
     A copy of [official record] dated [__] is annexed hereto and
     marked as ANNEXURE-R-2.

8.   [Continue, one reply paragraph for each material petition
     paragraph. Never leave a petition paragraph unanswered.]

9.   With reference to the allegation that no hearing was given,
     I say that notice dated [__] was served on [mode + date] and
     the Petitioner [appeared / remained absent / filed a reply
     dated __]. Copies are annexed as ANNEXURE-R-3 (Colly).

10.  With reference to the allegation of prejudice, I say that
     [facts].


                    REPLY TO THE GROUNDS

11.  The grounds of the Petition are denied. Without prejudice:

     a)    The [Respondent] acted within [section] of [statute].
           The ingredients of that section were satisfied because
           [facts already pleaded].

     b)    Natural justice was complied with as stated above.
           [Or: the statute prescribes a post-decisional hearing
           under section __, which remains open.]

     c)    Article 14 is not attracted. The classification /
           decision rests on [intelligible differentia / recorded
           reasons].

     d)    [Answer each lettered ground of the Petition by its
           letter.]


                    ADDITIONAL FACTS
                    (if the reply must put a positive case)

12.  I say that [positive case of the authority / private
     respondent — title, inspection, earlier consent, parallel
     proceeding, payment default, etc.].
     A copy of [document] is annexed as ANNEXURE-R-4.


                           PRAYER

     It is therefore prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased
     to:

     a)    dismiss the Writ Petition with costs;

     b)    vacate any ad-interim / interim relief granted on
           [date];

     c)    pass such other order as this Hon'ble Court may deem
           fit and proper.


Place: [PLACE]
Date:  [__].[__].20__

                                              [SIGNATURE OF DEPONENT]
                                              [Name, designation]


                           VERIFICATION

I, the deponent above-named, do hereby verify that what is stated
in paragraphs [1 to __] is true to my personal knowledge, that
what is stated in paragraphs [__ to __] is based on the official
record which I believe to be true, and that what is stated by way
of legal submission is so stated.

Solemnly affirmed at [Place]
this [__] day of [Month], 20__

                                              [SIGNATURE OF DEPONENT]

Identified / Before me
                                              [Advocate / Notary]


Note for Government replies: where the deponent is a public servant
affirming from the file, say so. Do not have a junior officer deny
a fact that only the file-holder can speak to.
```
