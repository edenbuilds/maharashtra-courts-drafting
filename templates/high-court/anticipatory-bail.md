# Anticipatory Bail — BNSS s.482 / CrPC s.438 (ABA)

If the Sessions Court has already refused, annex that order. The High Court application is a fresh ABA, not an appeal.

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]
[OR: IN THE COURT OF THE SESSIONS JUDGE AT [PLACE]]

CRIMINAL APPLICATION NO.             OF 20__

[Applicant]                                          } … Applicant

                ///VERSUS///

The State of Maharashtra                             } … Respondent

        APPLICATION FOR ANTICIPATORY BAIL
        UNDER [SECTION 482 OF THE BNSS / SECTION 438 OF THE CrPC]


1.   The Applicant apprehends arrest in FIR / CR No. [__]
     registered at [PS] on [date] for [sections]. ANNEXURE-A.

2.   The Applicant is [age, occupation, roots in the district,
     passport status].

3.   Prosecution allegations in brief, followed by the
     Applicant's version. Do not argue the trial.

4.   The Applicant [cooperated / is ready to cooperate] with
     the investigation. [Notice under BNSS s.35(3) / CrPC
     s.41-A, if any, and the reply.]

5.   Flight risk, tampering and the nature of the offence:
     [facts].

6.   Earlier ABA before the Sessions Court: [filed on __,
     rejected on __ / not filed]. ANNEXURE-B.

                             PRAYER

a)   direct that in the event of arrest in FIR / CR No. [__]
     of [PS], the Applicant be released on bail on such
     conditions as this Hon'ble Court may impose;
b)   ad-interim protection from arrest pending this Application;
c)   any other order as may be deemed fit.

Usual conditions to offer: attend PS as directed, not leave
Maharashtra without leave, not contact the informant, surrender
passport if directed.
```
