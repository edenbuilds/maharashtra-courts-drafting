# Second Appeal — Section 100 CPC

The first page after the cause title must formulate the substantial question(s) of law. No second appeal on pure fact.

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

SECOND APPEAL NO.                    OF 20__

[Appellant]                                          } … Appellant

                ///VERSUS///

[Respondent]                                         } … Respondent

        SECOND APPEAL UNDER SECTION 100 OF THE
        CODE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE, 1908


SUBSTANTIAL QUESTIONS OF LAW

1.   Whether the First Appellate Court erred in law in holding
     that [proposition], contrary to [section / binding principle]?
2.   Whether the finding of [X] is perverse, in that it ignores
     [admitted document / binding admission]?

[Do not list "the courts below erred on facts" as a question.]

MEMO, FACTS (compressed: two decrees, not a third trial),
GROUNDS tied to the questions, PRAYER to set aside the first
appellate decree and restore / modify as indicated.

Limitation and court-fee block as in First Appeal.
```
