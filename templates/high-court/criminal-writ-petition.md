# Criminal Writ Petition — Articles 226 / 227 and 21

Use for habeas corpus, preventive detention, police inaction, or quashing when the brief insists on a writ rather than BNSS s.528 / CrPC s.482.

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR]
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY
BENCH AT [NAGPUR / AURANGABAD / KOLHAPUR].

CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

CRIMINAL WRIT PETITION NO.           OF 20__

DISTRICT : [__]

[Name, age, occupation, address]                     }   … Petitioner

                ///VERSUS///

1.  The State of Maharashtra
    Through the [Public Prosecutor / Additional PP]
2.  The [Commissioner of Police / Superintendent of Police], [Place]
3.  The Senior Inspector of Police, [Police Station]
4.  [Complainant, if a necessary party]
                                                             }   … Respondents

        CRIMINAL WRIT PETITION
        UNDER ARTICLES 226 AND 227 READ WITH ARTICLE 21
        OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

TO
THE HON'BLE THE CHIEF JUSTICE AND THE OTHER HON'BLE PUISNE JUDGES

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHEWETH:

1.   The Petitioner is [accused / detenue / informant / next friend
     of the detenue]. FIR / CR No. [Case-No-Placeholder] was
     registered at [PS] on [date] for [sections of BNS / IPC].
     A copy of the FIR is annexed as ANNEXURE-A.

2.   [If detention: the detention order dated __ under [COFEPOSA /
     MPDA / NSA / other] is annexed as ANNEXURE-A. Grounds of
     detention are ANNEXURE-B.]

                            F A C T S

3–12.[Chronology. Service of grounds. Representation. Non-placement
     of a document before the Advisory Board. Alibi documents.
     Civil overlay. Settlement, if genuine and the offence is
     compoundable or falls in a Bhajan Lal category.]

13.  The Petitioner has not filed any other proceeding for the
     same relief except [Sessions ABA / earlier writ — give number].

                           G R O U N D S

A.   [Habeas: the detention order is vitiated for [non-application
     of mind / stale grounds / vital document withheld].]
B.   [Quashing: the FIR even if taken at face value does not
     disclose [offence], and falls in Bhajan Lal category [__].
     Do not invent a category.]
C.   [Police inaction: representation dated __ is unanswered.
     Mandamus is sought to [register / investigate / protect].]
D.   Article 21 is violated because [liberty interest actually
     at stake].

                             PRAYER

a)   [release the detenue forthwith] / [quash FIR / CR No. __
     and all proceedings arising therefrom] / [direct registration
     of FIR and investigation];
b)   pending hearing, [stay arrest / stay investigation /
     produce the detenue];
c)   ad-interim in terms of (b);
d)   any other order as may be deemed fit.

Place / Date / Counsel block / Verification.
```
