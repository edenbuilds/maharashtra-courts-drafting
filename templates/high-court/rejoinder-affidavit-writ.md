# Rejoinder Affidavit of the Petitioner

Use only after an Affidavit in Reply has been filed. A rejoinder answers **new** facts and **new** preliminary objections. It is not a second petition and it does not repeat the writ.

If the reply has not added anything new, tell the advocate. Many Bombay benches discourage a rejoinder that is only rhetoric.

---

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

WRIT PETITION NO. [____] OF 20__

[Petitioner]                                         … Petitioner

                ///VERSUS///

[Respondents]                                        … Respondents


        REJOINDER AFFIDAVIT
        ON BEHALF OF THE PETITIONER
        TO THE AFFIDAVIT IN REPLY OF RESPONDENT NO. [__]
        DATED [__]


I, [Name], the Petitioner above-named / authorised signatory of
the Petitioner, do hereby solemnly affirm and state as under:


1.   I have read the Affidavit in Reply dated [__] filed by
     Respondent No. [__] ("the Reply"). I confine this rejoinder
     to the new assertions and the preliminary objections. I do
     not repeat the Petition.

2.   Save what is specifically admitted below, I deny the
     contents of the Reply.


              PRELIMINARY OBJECTIONS ANSWERED

3.   As to alternative remedy: the Reply relies on [section].
     That remedy is not equally efficacious because [reason
     already in the Petition, now tied to the record produced
     with the Reply].

4.   As to delay: certified copy was received on [__] /
     the action is a continuing one because [__] /
     the Petitioner first made a representation on [__].

5.   As to suppression: the document now produced as
     ANNEXURE-R-[__] was [not in the Petitioner's possession /
     is being explained as follows]. A copy of [explaining
     document], if any, is annexed as ANNEXURE-P-[next letter].

6.   As to disputed facts: the dispute now raised is [not a
     genuine dispute / is concluded by ANNEXURE-__].


              REPLY TO NEW FACTS

7.   With reference to paragraph [__] of the Reply, I say that
     [the date / service / hearing asserted is incorrect because …].

8.   With reference to ANNEXURE-R-[__], I say that [the document
     does not bear acknowledgement / is an internal note / does
     not support the conclusion drawn].

9.   [Further new facts only.]


              PRAYER

     The Petitioner reiterates the prayers in the Writ Petition
     and prays that the Petition be made absolute.

Place: [PLACE]
Date:  [__].[__].20__

                                              [SIGNATURE OF DEPONENT]


                           VERIFICATION

[Standard first-person verification, split between personal
knowledge and record.]
```
