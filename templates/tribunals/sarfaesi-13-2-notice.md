# Demand Notice — SARFAESI s.13(2)

Bank-side statutory notice. Sixty-day window. Refer to s.13(3A).

```
By Registered Post AD / Speed Post / email
Ref. No. [__]                 Date: [__]

To
1.  [Borrower]
2.  [Guarantor]
3.  [Mortgagor]

        NOTICE UNDER SECTION 13(2) OF THE
        SECURITISATION AND RECONSTRUCTION OF FINANCIAL
        ASSETS AND ENFORCEMENT OF SECURITY INTEREST ACT, 2002


1.   You availed [facility] from [Bank], [Branch], under
     sanction letter dated [__]. The facility is secured by
     [description of security].

2.   The account was classified as a non-performing asset on
     [date] in accordance with RBI directions.

3.   The outstanding as on [date] is Rs. [Amount] together with
     further interest, costs and charges.

4.   You are hereby called upon to discharge the entire
     liability within sixty days from the date of this notice,
     failing which the undersigned as Authorised Officer shall
     proceed under s.13(4) of the Act.

5.   You may make a representation under s.13(3A) within the
     statutory period. Reasons will be given if the
     representation is rejected.

6.   This notice is issued without prejudice to other remedies
     including the pending / proposed OA before the DRT.

                                              [Name]
                                              Authorised Officer
                                              [Bank]
Schedule of secured asset: [full description]
```
