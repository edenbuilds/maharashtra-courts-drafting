# MACT Claim Petition — Section 166, Motor Vehicles Act

Maharashtra practice uses a 22-item tabular block plus a short narrative.

```
BEFORE THE HON'BLE MOTOR ACCIDENT CLAIMS TRIBUNAL
AT [DISTRICT], MAHARASHTRA

M.A.C.P. NO.                         OF 20__

1.  [Claimant No. 1 — widow / injured — age, occupation, address]
2.  [Minor claimant, through next friend]
                                                     } … Claimants

                       VERSUS

1.  [Driver of the offending vehicle], age, address
2.  [Owner], address
3.  [Insurer — full corporate name and registered / servicing office]
                                                     } … Respondents

        CLAIM PETITION UNDER SECTION 166
        OF THE MOTOR VEHICLES ACT, 1988


                    TABULAR PARTICULARS

 1. Name and address of claimant(s) with relationship
 2. Age of claimant(s)
 3. Occupation of claimant(s)
 4. Name, age, occupation and monthly income of deceased / injured
 5. Date, time and place of accident
 6. Name and address of the driver
 7. Name and address of the owner
 8. Registration number, make, model, year of the offending vehicle
 9. Insurer; policy number; period of validity
10. RTO of registration; permit-area (commercial vehicles)
11. FIR number, date and Police Station
12. Charge-sheet / final report status
13. Brief description of the accident
14. Nature and extent of injury / death
15. Hospitalisation; treating hospital(s)
16. Permanent disability percentage (with certificate)
17. Documentary basis for income
18. Dependants and relationship
19. Compensation claimed — head-wise
20. Total compensation claimed: Rs. [Amount]
21. Interest claimed: [__]% from [date of accident / filing]
22. Court fee paid / exemption applied for

NARRATIVE

1.   On [date] at about [time] at [place] the offending vehicle
     [MH-__-__] driven by Respondent No. 1, owned by Respondent
     No. 2 and insured with Respondent No. 3, [struck / ran over]
     the [deceased / injured]. FIR is ANNEXURE-A. Spot panchnama
     ANNEXURE-B. Policy ANNEXURE-C. PM / disability certificate
     ANNEXURE-D. Income proof ANNEXURE-E.

2.   The accident occurred solely due to the rash and negligent
     driving of Respondent No. 1. Vicarious liability of the
     owner and statutory liability of the insurer follow.

3.   Heads of claim (do not invent a multiplier; use the method
     the advocate has named):
     - Loss of dependency: Rs. [__]
     - Consortium / loss of love: Rs. [__]
     - Funeral / estate / conventional heads: Rs. [__]
     - Medical / special damages: Rs. [__]
     TOTAL: Rs. [__]

                             PRAYER

a)   award Rs. [__] with interest at [__]% per annum from [date]
     jointly and severally against the Respondents;
b)   direct the insurer to deposit the award;
c)   costs;
d)   any other order as may be deemed fit.

Verification of all adult claimants. Minor: next-friend affidavit.
```
