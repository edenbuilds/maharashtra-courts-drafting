# Reply to a statutory notice under Section 138 NI Act

Do not admit a legally enforceable debt by loose drafting.

```
By Registered Post AD / email
Without prejudice
Ref. [__]                 Date: [__]

To
[Advocate for the complainant]
[Address]

        REPLY TO YOUR NOTICE DATED [__]
        ALLEGING AN OFFENCE UNDER SECTION 138
        OF THE NEGOTIABLE INSTRUMENTS ACT, 1881


Sir,

Under instructions from my client [Name] I state as follows.

1.   My client denies that there exists a legally enforceable
     debt of Rs. [Amount] as alleged. The cheque bearing No.
     [__] dated [__] was [issued as security for __ / stolen /
     given for a transaction that has already been discharged
     by __].

2.   The allegations in paragraphs [__] of your notice are
     denied.

3.   [If part-payment: Rs. __ was paid on __ by UTR __, which
     your client has suppressed.]

4.   [If notice defective: the notice does not satisfy the
     proviso to s.138 because __.]

5.   My client is not liable to pay the sum demanded. Your
     client is called upon to withdraw the notice. Any
     complaint filed will be defended and costs will be
     claimed.

This reply is without prejudice to my client's rights and
defences.

                                              [Name], Advocate
                                              Enrolment No. MH/[__]/[__]
```
