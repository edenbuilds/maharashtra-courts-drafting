# Gift Deed — Maharashtra

Acceptance by the donee is essential. Registration is mandatory
for immovable property.

```
DEED OF GIFT

THIS DEED OF GIFT is made at [Place] on [date]

BETWEEN

[Donor] ("the Donor") of the ONE PART

AND

[Donee] ("the Donee") of the OTHER PART

WHEREAS the Donor is the absolute owner of the property
described in the Schedule.

AND WHEREAS the Donor, out of natural love and affection for
the Donee, being the Donor's [daughter / son / other], is
desirous of gifting the said property.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES:

1.   The Donor hereby gifts, transfers and conveys the
     property described in the Schedule to the Donee.

2.   The Donee hereby accepts the gift.

3.   Possession is delivered to the Donee on the date of
     this Deed.

4.   The gift is absolute and irrevocable.

5.   Stamp duty is paid under the live Maharashtra article
     applicable to a gift [to a family member / to a stranger]
     of [residential / other] property.

SCHEDULE OF PROPERTY
[as in the sale-deed form]

DONOR                          DONEE (ACCEPTANCE)
```
