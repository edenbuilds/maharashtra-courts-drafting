# Deed of Conveyance — Maharashtra

Stamp on the higher of consideration and ready-reckoner. Article
and rate move by notification. Do not print a stale percentage.

```
DEED OF CONVEYANCE

THIS DEED OF CONVEYANCE is made at [Place] on this [__] day
of [Month], 20__

BETWEEN

[Vendor — name, age, PAN, address, CIDCO / society status]
("the Vendor") of the ONE PART

AND

[Purchaser — name, age, PAN, address]
("the Purchaser") of the OTHER PART

WHEREAS the Vendor is the [owner / development-agreement holder]
of the property described in the Schedule, which stands in the
Vendor's name in [Property Card / 7/12 extract] and which is
free from encumbrance save [disclose].

AND WHEREAS the Purchaser has agreed to purchase and the Vendor
has agreed to sell the said property for a total consideration
of Rs. [Amount] (Rupees [__] only), which is not less than the
ready-reckoner value notified for [village / ward] for 20__-__.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES as follows:

1.   Transfer: the Vendor conveys unto the Purchaser the
     property described in the Schedule TO HOLD the same
     absolutely and forever.

2.   Consideration: Rs. [__] paid as follows — [token /
     agreement / this day by cheque / RTGS UTR __]. The
     Vendor admits receipt.

3.   Possession: vacant / symbolic possession is handed over
     on the date of this Deed.

4.   Title warranty: the Vendor has good right to convey and
     shall keep the Purchaser indemnified against defect in
     title up to this date.

5.   Outgoings: [taxes, society dues, CIDCO / MHADA charges]
     up to the date of this Deed are on the Vendor; thereafter
     on the Purchaser.

6.   Stamp and registration: borne by [Purchaser], computed on
     the higher of consideration and ready-reckoner.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this Deed.

SCHEDULE OF PROPERTY
[District, taluka, village, survey / CTS / HISSA, area,
boundaries, flat / unit no., floor, building, society,
carpet area.]

VENDOR                         PURCHASER
[signature]                    [signature]

WITNESSES: 1. ____    2. ____
```
