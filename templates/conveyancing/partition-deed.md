# Deed of Partition — Maharashtra

```
DEED OF PARTITION

THIS DEED OF PARTITION is made at [Place] on [date]

BETWEEN

1.  [Co-owner A]
2.  [Co-owner B]
3.  [Co-owner C]
     (collectively "the Parties")

WHEREAS the Parties are [coparceners / co-owners] of the
properties described in Schedule A, inherited from / purchased
by [common ancestor / joint purchase dated __].

AND WHEREAS the Parties have agreed to partition the said
properties by metes and bounds as set out in Schedules B, C
and D.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES:

1.   Schedule B is allotted to A; Schedule C to B; Schedule D
     to C, absolutely.

2.   Each Party releases the others in respect of the property
     allotted to them.

3.   Documents of title relating to each allotted item shall
     be handed over to the allottee. Common documents shall
     remain with [A] who shall produce them on demand.

4.   Stamp duty is paid on the non-family / non-coparcener
     slice, if any, as per the live Maharashtra article.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF …

SCHEDULE A — joint properties
SCHEDULE B / C / D — allotments
```
