# Application for suspension of sentence and bail pending criminal appeal

Statutory hook: BNSS s.430 / CrPC s.389, according to the date of the impugned judgment.

---

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

INTERIM APPLICATION NO.              OF 20__
IN
CRIMINAL APPEAL NO.                  OF 20__

[Convict-Appellant]                                  … Applicant

                ///VERSUS///

The State of Maharashtra                             … Respondent


        APPLICATION FOR SUSPENSION OF SENTENCE
        AND FOR BAIL PENDING APPEAL
        UNDER [SECTION 430 OF THE BNSS / SECTION 389 OF THE CrPC]


1.   The Applicant has filed the accompanying Criminal Appeal
     against the judgment and order dated [__] passed by the
     learned [Sessions / Special] Judge, [Place], in [Sessions
     Case No. __], convicting the Applicant for [offences] and
     sentencing the Applicant to [sentence].

2.   The Applicant has been in custody since [__]. The sentence
     remaining is [__].

3.   Prima facie: [the point that makes the appeal arguable —
     missing ingredient, hostile witnesses, sentence without
     reasons]. Do not retry the case here.

4.   The Applicant [has / has no] passport, [has / has no]
     criminal antecedents, and undertakes to attend on every
     date.

                             PRAYER

     a)    suspend the sentence imposed by the judgment dated
           [__] pending the hearing and final disposal of the
           accompanying Appeal;

     b)    enlarge the Applicant on bail on such conditions as
           this Hon'ble Court may impose;

     c)    pass such other order as may be deemed fit.

Place: [PLACE]
Date:  [__].[__].20__

                                              ([NAME], Advocate)

                    AFFIDAVIT IN SUPPORT
[First-person, or a relative if the Applicant is in jail — and
then pray that the Applicant's personal affidavit be dispensed
with.]
```
