# Interim / Stay Application (travels with a Writ or First Appeal)

Separate petition. Own cause title, own facts (short), own prayer, own affidavit. Do not bury stay in a paragraph of the main pleading.

---

```
IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT BOMBAY.
[OR bench line]

CIVIL APPELLATE JURISDICTION

INTERIM APPLICATION NO.              OF 20__
IN
WRIT PETITION / FIRST APPEAL NO.     OF 20__

[Applicant / Petitioner]                             … Applicant

                ///VERSUS///

[Respondents]                                        … Respondents


        APPLICATION FOR INTERIM RELIEF
        [STAY OF THE IMPUGNED ORDER DATED [__]]


THE APPLICANT ABOVENAMED MOST RESPECTFULLY SHEWETH:

1.   The Applicant has filed the accompanying [Writ Petition /
     First Appeal] challenging [impugned order] dated [__].
     The contents of the accompanying proceeding are adopted
     and are not repeated.

2.   Prima facie case: [one short paragraph — the jurisdictional
     or natural-justice defect that is visible on the annexures].

3.   Balance of convenience: [what happens if the order operates
     this week — demolition, recovery, termination, eviction,
     blacklisting].

4.   Irreparable injury: [why money or a later appeal will not
     repair it].

5.   The Applicant is ready to abide by any condition this
     Hon'ble Court may impose, including [deposit / undertaking
     / status-quo on construction / weekly listing].

                             PRAYER

     a)    stay the effect, operation and implementation of the
           [order / decree / notice] dated [__] pending the
           hearing and final disposal of the accompanying
           [Petition / Appeal];

     b)    grant ad-interim relief in terms of prayer clause (a);

     c)    pass such other order as may be deemed fit.

Place: [PLACE]
Date:  [__].[__].20__

                                              ([NAME], Advocate)
                                              Advocate for the Applicant


                    AFFIDAVIT IN SUPPORT

I, [Name], the Applicant, do hereby solemnly affirm that what is
stated in paragraphs 1 to 5 is true to my knowledge and that
the prayer is for the ends of justice.

Solemnly affirmed at [Place] on [date]
                                              [SIGNATURE]
```
